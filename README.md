# Choose buildings for an energy review

Energy teams cannot inspect every property at once. This study tests a short review list based on a property's energy use relative to comparable buildings, alongside lists based on total consumption and energy intensity alone.

The intended use is to decide where to check meter data, operating schedules and equipment first. A flag is a reason to investigate. It does not establish wasted energy, an equipment fault, a compliance failure or achievable savings.
