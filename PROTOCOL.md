# Building energy review: fixed protocol before outcome collection

## Decision and acceptance

Help an energy team choose a limited set of commercial buildings for a closer review. Compare a peer-relative EUI ranking with the largest-energy and highest-EUI lists at the same capacities. The key practical output is a reproducible shortlist with each reason and each excluded record visible. Prediction error, list overlap and next-year reporting/persistence describe this public-data exercise; engineering usefulness, faults, waste, compliance and achievable savings are unknown. No monetary or causal impact is claimed.

This scoped design uses simple median peer references. The broader proposal of GAM/boosting/R and independent weather modeling is historical context, not executed work. Published weather-normalized EUI is inherited from Portfolio Manager; this study does not validate or refit its normalization. Accept the scoped release only with reconciled source fields, explicit coverage, independent arithmetic/lineage, all candidate results, a frozen selection before held summaries, finite boundary tests, offline replay, inspected figures/paper, plain first-layer use and limitations, bounded privacy checks and exact-head Linux/remote release proof. At most three review rounds, 45 active review minutes and 60 review-specific calls; no judge-only verification.

## Eligibility and identity, fixed before scores

Preserve all retrieved rows and all exclusion reasons; never turn Not Available, missing, Unable to Check or Possible Issue into zero. Eligible row rules: integer year2022..2024; year_ending has that year's December31 date; one seven-digit NYC BIN starting1..5; numeric positive property ID; standalone parent token exactly Not Applicable: Standalone Property; construction Existing; number_of_buildings exactly1; occupancy80..100%; self-reported GFA25000..5000000ft2 and building-calculated GFA positive within5% of self-reported; finite positive weather-normalized EUI<=2000kBtu/ft2/year, finite positive site EUI and site energy; estimated_values_energy exactly No; all four selected alert fields exactly Ok. These cohort bounds and quality tokens are declared screening assumptions, not empirically tuned physical laws or verified measurement accuracy.

Require abs(site_EUI - site_energy/GFA)<=max(0.2,0.01*site_EUI) kBtu/ft2/year, allowing bounded reported rounding. Both raw values remain available; this does not validate meters. A year/BIN with multiple retrieved records is excluded in full, including identical duplicates, to avoid row-order choices. A property ID linked to more than one BIN across the snapshot excludes all its records. BIN controls entity grouping and joins. One row per eligible year/BIN is required. Exclusions are part of the result.

## Entity and calendar separation

Assign each BIN using int(SHA256(BIN),16)%5. Buckets0,1,2 supply only eligible2022 training rows. Bucket3 supplies validation pairs: eligible2022 inputs and eligible2023 target, stable primary type and following/input self GFA ratio0.8..1.25. Bucket4 supplies held pairs: eligible2023 inputs and eligible2024 target under the same rules. No BIN can cross roles. Feature inputs are previous-year property type/GFA; target is next-year published weather-normalized site EUI. Require >=100 total training rows and >=50 pairs in each validation and held partition, otherwise retain a coverage hold without changing splits/bounds. No minimum per subgroup licenses removing a subgroup.

All source inputs and quality fields are unblinded; held scores and summaries are computed only after the selected configuration, trained medians, source and executable dependencies are hashed and committed. Current revised input records are assumed available in an idealized prior-year retrospective experiment; no prospective forecast claim.

## Peer models and baselines

Training medians are exact rational medians of2022 bucket0..2 EUI. A pooled median is candidate0. Five further candidates blend a within-type/size-bin median with the type median using size-bin weight0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1. Area bins have edges25000,50000,100000,250000,500000,1000000,5000001ft2, left-closed/right-open. A type median needs>=20 training records, else pooled. A type/area-bin median needs>=20 records, else its type fallback. All fallback counts/levels are exported. Weight0 is the type-only peer reference. No fitted neural, GAM or boosting model and no optimizer are used.

Choose among these six peer configurations by equal-building validation MAE in kBtu/ft2/year; exact ties use the lower candidate index. Report MAE,MSE,RMSE and GFA-weighted MAE plus all property-type results. Retain every attempted candidate. The own-prior-year EUI persistence forecast is a fixed comparator, reported in validation and held but excluded from selecting a structural peer reference: it repeats a property's own level rather than providing independent peer context. The selected peer model is not claimed to beat persistence, be globally optimal, or estimate causal efficiency.

## Review lists and next-year follow-up

Construct review queues from every eligible bucket4 current2023 row, before requiring2024 follow-up. Rank three ways: current weather-normalized EUI / selected frozen peer reference; current reported annual site energy; current weather-normalized EUI. Descending score ties use ascending full building hash. Capacities are ceil5%,ceil10%,ceil20% of this same full queue, with at least one row if nonempty. The10% list is the main illustration; these are hypothetical capacity scenarios, not observed staffing budgets. Export all ranks and list memberships, sizes, pairwise overlap/Jaccard, median current floor area and per-type composition.

Match2024 follow-up separately, using the held pair rules. Report missing/invalid/type-change/area-change follow-up for every selected list, preserving the full selected denominator. For follow-up only, summarize the next-year EUI divided by the same frozen peer prediction based on2023 features, including median ratio and fractions above1.25,1.5,2. These fixed relative-use bands describe persistence; they are not calibrated waste/defect probabilities, independent engineering labels or savings targets. Report own-year residual signs and all local failures. No tuning against the held results or queue follow-up. Do not compare strategies as if selective follow-up or future stable reporters represent all buildings.
