# Source and publication boundary

Source: NYC Department of Buildings, [LL84 annual disclosure 5zyy-y8am](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Environment/NYC-Building-Energy-and-Water-Data-Disclosure-for-/5zyy-y8am). [NYC Open Data terms](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/stories/s/Terms-of-Use/k9k7-3cje) describe informational public records, agency ownership of source quality/version control, no accuracy/fitness warranty and possible corrections or overwriting. The [City's public-data policy](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/citypolicies.html) describes the open-by-default program. Dataset licenseId is null; this repository claims neither a CC0 license nor copyright ownership of City content.

Publish minimal factual quantitative/token projections with attribution and hashed source identifiers, plus original code, calculations and reviewed independent figures/manuscript. Full selected responses, BIN/property identity crosswalks, documentation and unused fields stay private. No owner/contact/address/coordinate/narrative fields are requested. Hashes do not guarantee anonymity for public small-domain identifiers. Formal-publication projects and confidential, clinical or private personal/business data are excluded.

[EPA defines EUI](https://www.energystar.gov/buildings/benchmark/understand-metrics/what-eui) as annual energy divided by floor area and explains why property types differ. The published weather-normalized EUI is inherited; no independent weather fit, physical engineering validation or utility-bill audit is claimed. Use current public measurements as reported, preserving missing tokens and all exclusions.
